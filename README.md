## About the Research

LifeLink is a hospital-centered digital platform designed to improve organ and blood donation coordination through donor management, recipient requests, compatibility-based matching, and privacy-conscious information handling.

## Research Focus

- Organ donor management
- Blood donor management
- Donor-recipient coordination
- Hospital-centered donor registration
- AI-assisted healthcare decision support
- Privacy and secure donor information management
